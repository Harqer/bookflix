INT. ANCIENT LABORATORY - NIGHT

A heavy brass sphere (approx 50kg) sits atop a friction-less obsidian table. 
The lighting is dim, provided by a flickering blue plasma arc in the center of the room.

The atmosphere is thick with static electricity. 

The sphere begins to vibrate—a resonance frequency that matches the plasma arc.
Suddenly, the sphere slides across the table, accelerating at exactly 2m/s^2. 
As it reaches the edge, it doesn't fall. It orbits the plasma arc in a perfect 4D spiral, 
defying standard 3D gravity but obeying a complex magnetic Hamiltonian.

The camera orbits the sphere as it moves, maintaining perfect focus on the brass texture.
